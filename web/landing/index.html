Countersign Sandbox demo

Let any PayPal merchant accept AI-agent purchases, with cryptographic proof of what the customer approved, enforced at the moment PayPal captures the money.

Countersign sits in front of a merchant's PayPal Orders integration. It checks the customer's signed mandates before a PayPal authorization, and it re-reads the live PayPal order before every capture, voiding the authorization if anything differs from what was signed.

It also keeps a dispute evidence bundle for each capture that can be verified offline. This is PayPal sandbox only. Orders marked simulator or recorded in the dashboard are exactly that: made over an in-memory PayPal simulator, or replayed from one real recorded sandbox capture. Nothing here is a live payment.

What you can try

  • Merchant dashboard

    The orders grid, an order's signed checkout against the live PayPal order, evidence, protection results and the assistant. Rows are labelled simulator or recorded.

  • Evidence verifier, with a one-click try-it

    Loads a sample evidence bundle, checks all twelve steps in your browser, then lets you change one field and see exactly which step fails.

  • UCP discovery profile

    The merchant's public profile an agent reads first: the checkout capability, the AP2 mandates extension and the signing key.

  • Health

    The running mode and whether any protection is switched off.

    MIT licensed. How to run it locally, the evaluation results and the limitations.
